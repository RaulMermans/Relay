---
name: sprint-close
description: Use before marking a Relay sprint complete. Do not use while its acceptance criteria or required verification remain incomplete.
---

## Goal

Confirm the sprint's documented reality matches its verified outcome.

## Read first

`CLAUDE.md`, current `plan.md`, the relevant roadmap entry, changelog, scratchpad, ADRs, and verification evidence.

## Workflow

Verify all task criteria, run appropriate checks, align docs with implemented reality, update roadmap/changelog/scratchpad, confirm required ADRs, and list unresolved debt.

## Constraints

Do not claim evidence that does not exist or close a sprint with unacknowledged gaps.

## Output

Pass/blocked status, verification evidence, documentation updates, unresolved items, and next task.
