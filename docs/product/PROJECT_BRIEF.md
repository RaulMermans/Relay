# Relay project brief

**Name:** Relay
**Archetype:** MVP

## Users and problem

Relay serves freelance performance marketers and small performance marketing agencies. Their recurring client reports require repetitive manual work across fragmented marketing and commerce data.

## User story

As a performance marketer, I want to connect my platforms or upload exports and receive a validated, decision-ready report so I can spend less time rebuilding reporting every week or month.

## Core sources

- Meta Ads
- Google Ads
- Shopify

## Core workflow

Ingestion -> normalization -> reconciliation -> KPI computation -> change intelligence -> grounded narrative -> human review -> PDF report.

## Initial success targets to validate

These are hypotheses, not evidence, until beta measurement exists:

- At least 60% median reporting-time reduction
- At least 70% of generated insights accepted without major correction
- Repeated use across multiple reporting cycles
