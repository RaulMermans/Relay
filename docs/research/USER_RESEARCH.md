# User research protocol

## Goal

Run 3-5 structured research sessions with performance marketers or small-agency operators to determine whether recurring reporting workflows are similar, painful, and reducible enough to validate Relay's product contract.

## Participants and consent

Assign each participant a sequential ID (`R-001`, `R-002`, and so on). Record their consent to discuss their workflow and their permission level for each artifact. Do not store real client names unless anonymized.

## Session procedure

1. Assign the participant ID and collect the profile below.
2. Ask the participant to walk through their most recent recurring report step by step.
3. Time or reconstruct the workflow stages and capture their current inputs and outputs.
4. Ask the pain and trust questions without leading the participant.
5. Record what can be standardized, what requires client-specific memory, and what needs human judgment.
6. Remove sensitive details before retaining research evidence in the repository.

## Profile

Capture role, freelancer or agency status, number of clients, reporting cadence, platforms used, and typical report format.

## Workflow measurement

Document the current reporting process step by step. Capture total reporting minutes and, where distinguishable, exporting, cleaning, reconciliation, KPI/calculation, interpretation, formatting, and QA/checking minutes.

## Inputs

Where permission allows, collect anonymized current-period exports, previous-period exports, a completed report, KPI definitions, target values, and client-specific reporting rules.

## Pain and trust questions

- Which step takes the longest, is most repetitive, or is most error-prone?
- Where do you double-check numbers, and what do you still calculate manually?
- What requires screenshots or copying between tools?
- What would you never trust automation to do?
- What client context cannot be inferred from platform data?
- What would make you use Relay every reporting cycle, or make you stop using it?

Avoid leading prompts such as, "Wouldn't this save you time?"

## Privacy and evidence handling

- Anonymize clients and remove emails and personal names.
- Remove credentials and tokens.
- Do not commit sensitive exports without explicit anonymization.
- Retain only evidence required for product validation.

## Session output

For each participant, record the profile, workflow timing, inputs available, pains, trust boundaries, repeat-use conditions, and open questions. A researcher unfamiliar with Relay should be able to run this protocol and produce that record without additional instructions.
