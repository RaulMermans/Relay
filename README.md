# Relay

> Connect your marketing platforms or upload your exports. Relay turns the data into a decision-ready client report.

## Problem

Performance marketers repeatedly collect exports, clean fields, reconcile reporting periods, calculate KPIs, identify drivers, write commentary, and rebuild client reports.

## Product

Relay automates that reporting workflow while keeping data verification and human review in the loop.

## Initial users

- Freelance performance marketers
- Small performance marketing agencies
